What is Vigil?

Overview

Vigil is a tool developed to help IT Security professionals for small organizations.

Purpose

The purpose of the app is for teams to upload log files and analyze them in order to detect irregular or suspicious activity within their organization's IT infrastructure. They will be able to generate reports that can be seen by other people on the team in order to make the entirety of the team aware of a potential security issue.

Team

Vigil is developed by Marvin Rhodes, Kyle Cooper, and Tanner Benson
